## 👋 About Me

I'm a **Senior Data Engineer / Data Warehouse Analyst at Commercial International Bank (CIB)**, where I design and maintain enterprise data warehouse solutions, ETL pipelines and BI reporting that support business decision-making.

I hold an **MEng in Data Science and AI from the University of Ottawa** (graduated with *Excellent*) and a B.Sc. in Computer Science from Menoufia University. My background combines **data engineering, business and data analysis, and applied AI**, including machine learning, NLP and computer vision.

I enjoy turning business requirements into reliable data models, clean pipelines and clear dashboards.
